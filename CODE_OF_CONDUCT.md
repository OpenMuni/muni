# Code of Conduct

Muni is a civic project. People who live with the consequences of public data
should be able to help shape how it is collected and explained. We welcome
contributors with different technical backgrounds, identities, and views.

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking
people. Make room for questions and corrections. Do not harass, threaten,
discriminate against, or disclose private information about another person.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces.

If you experience or witness a problem, email
[hello@muni.fyi](mailto:hello@muni.fyi) with `Muni community conduct` in
the subject. Please do not put a sensitive report in a public issue.
Maintainers will review reports privately, consider the safety of the people
involved, and may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict
participation. We will avoid sharing a reporter's identity without their
permission unless necessary to address an immediate safety concern.
